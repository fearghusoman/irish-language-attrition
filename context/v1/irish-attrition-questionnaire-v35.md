## Notes for the collaborator on scoring

**The 4 core predictors entering the regression model:**
1. Proficiency (Q12–Q14, CAO-points-by-year conversion; Q13 fallback for those who didn't sit the Leaving Cert)
2. Exposure to Irish since the end of formal instruction (Q16–Q20, averaged composite)
3. Integrative/cultural motivation (Q21–Q24, averaged composite)
4. Multilingualism (Q28, count of currently actively used additional languages — rated "Occasionally," "Often," or "Daily/almost daily")

**Background-only composites**: Q36–38 (attitudes), Q39–42 (learning experience), Q43–44 (classroom anxiety), Q45–48 (parental encouragement), Q51–52 (linguistic identity now) — average within each group separately, don't mix across constructs. Q25–26 (instrumental motivation), Q53 (self-rated attrition), Q54 (reconnection interest), and Q27 (confidence, covers all motivation items 21–26) are each their own single composite/item — kept separate. Q49–50 (family Irish proficiency) are standalone Yes/No factual items — do NOT average into the parental encouragement Likert composite (Q45–48), report separately. Q33–35 (time lived abroad) are standalone descriptive items — Q34 (years abroad) and Q35 (access while abroad) only apply if Q33 = Yes; not part of any composite.

**Note on instrumental motivation (Q25–26)**: not part of the primary 4-predictor model, but used in a separate, explicitly-labelled secondary/exploratory 5-predictor model — see codebook Section F for the full analysis plan.
